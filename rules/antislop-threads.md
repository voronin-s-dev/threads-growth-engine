# 🚫 Anti-Slop and Human Voice Standard for Threads

> **Purpose:** Strict ban on synthetic AI writing patterns, corporate buzzwords, and fake engagement bait.

---

## 1. Strictly Forbidden Opening Patterns

1. "In today's fast-paced world..."
2. "Let's dive in..."
3. "Here is the ultimate guide to..."
4. "Ever wondered why..."
5. "В современном мире..."
6. "Давайте разберемся..."
7. "Мало кто задумывается, но..."
8. "Вы когда-нибудь замечали..."

---

## 2. Strictly Forbidden Structural Tells

1. The "It's not X, it's Y" cliché ("Дело не в лени, дело в...").
2. The "No X, no Y, no Z" list pattern.
3. Excessive emoji lists acting as bullet points (🚀, 💡, 🔥, ✨).
4. Preachy coaching imperatives ("Перестаньте терпеть", "Запомните раз и навсегда").
5. Artificial engagement endings ("Согласны? Напишите в комментариях", "Ставьте лайк если полезно").

---

## 3. Human Tone Anchors

1. The author uses grounded everyday physical details instead of abstract metaphors.
2. The author uses conversational cadence with natural rhythm variations.
3. The author expresses calm conviction without shouting or sensationalism.
4. The author allows open-ended nuance instead of black-and-white absolute claims.
