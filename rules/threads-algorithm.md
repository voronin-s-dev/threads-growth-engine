# 🧵 Threads Algorithm and Publishing Standard

> **Purpose:** Universal operational rules for audience growth on Threads.

---

## 1. Publishing Schedule and Cadence

1. The author publishes two to three anchor threads weekly.
2. The author publishes one or two short standalone posts daily.
3. Every anchor thread contains four to five connected posts.
4. Every standalone post contains one to three sentences.
5. The author replies to all comments within 45 minutes of publishing.
6. The algorithm rewards immediate creator engagement with expanded reach.

---

## 2. Platform Character Limits

1. Threads enforces a technical limit of 500 characters per post.
2. Threads enforces a technical limit of 500 characters per comment.
3. The agent verifies length before presenting any draft.
4. The agent keeps standalone posts between 150 and 350 characters for readability.

---

## 3. Formatting and Paragraph Layout

1. The author writes compact paragraphs of two to three sentences.
2. The author avoids single-line sentences separated by blank lines.
3. The author avoids large blocks of uninterrupted text.
4. The author uses bullet points only for action steps.
5. The author never places external links in Post 1.
6. External links belong in the profile biography or in Post 5.

---

## 4. Algorithmic Ranking Signals

1. The algorithm prioritizes posts with high comment velocity.
2. The algorithm values replies and reposts over passive likes.
3. The author sparks conversation through authentic tension or counter-intuitive observations.
4. The author avoids synthetic engagement bait like generic surveys.
