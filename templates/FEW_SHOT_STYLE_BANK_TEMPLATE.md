## Template Pair 01: The Paradox Hook

### Source Viral Post (Deconstructed):
> *«Most people think poor sleep comes from screen time. But after tracking 400 clients, the real culprit was eating dinner 90 minutes before bed. Here is the biochemical breakdown.»*

* **Hook Syntax:** `[Common belief] + [Real world counter-evidence] + [Actual cause]`.
* **Psychological Tension:** Cognitive dissonance between popular belief and real data.
* **Discussion Trigger:** Readers immediately compare their own habits in comments.

### Adapted Niche Formula:
```markdown
[Most people believe X causes problem Y.
After testing/observing Z cases, the actual bottleneck was W.
Here is what is happening under the hood.]
```

## Template Pair 02: The Cost of Waiting

### Source Viral Post (Deconstructed):
> *«Staying at a job you hate does not just cost you happiness. It costs you $18,000 a year in delayed skill compounding. The math nobody likes to run:»*

* **Hook Syntax:** `[Common passive behavior] + [Surprise hidden financial/time cost] + [Invitation to examine numbers]`.
* **Psychological Tension:** Loss aversion and regret avoidance.
* **Discussion Trigger:** Readers calculate their personal numbers and debate in comments.

### Adapted Niche Formula:
```markdown
[Delaying decision X does not just waste time.
It quietly costs you [surprising hidden metric].
Here is the math:]
```
